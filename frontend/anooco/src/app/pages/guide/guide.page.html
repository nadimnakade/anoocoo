User Guide

Welcome to Anooco

Your intelligent road companion. Stay safe, informed, and connected.

Dashboard & Map

The Map: Shows your current location and nearby alerts (potholes, accidents, police, etc.).

View Modes: Use the Map / List / Split button in the bottom actions to toggle between:

  • Split: Map on top, alerts list below.
  • Map Only: Full-screen map (default when you open the app).
  • List Only: Full-screen list of alerts.

Settings & Tools: Use the gear icon in the top-left to open an action sheet for Settings and Tools.

Alerts Icon: The bell icon in the top-right opens the alerts list. The badge shows how many active alerts are nearby.

 Reporting Incidents

Voice Reporting: Tap the Voice button in the bottom actions, the assistant mic on the map, or say a wake word (if Hands-free is on). Then say for example:

  • "Report accident"
  • "Report pothole"
  • "Report traffic"
  • "Report police"

Wake Words: With Hands-free on, the app listens in the background for "Hey Anooco", "Hey Scout" or "Hey Echo". Only after a wake word does it fully listen to your command, so normal conversation does not create reports.

Manual Reporting: Tap the "Manual" button to select an incident type from the grid.

Hands-free Mode: Tap "Auto On/Off" to enable background listening for wake words. Ideal for driving.

Emergency Beam (Ambulance): After a wake word (e.g. "Hey Scout"), say something like "Beam emergency ahead" to send an emergency vehicle alert from your current location so other drivers can give way.

Quick Start for Ambulance Drivers:

  • Before moving, turn Auto On so the app listens for wake words.
  • On the road, say "Hey Scout" or "Hey Echo" to wake the assistant.
  • Immediately follow with "Beam emergency ahead" to warn nearby drivers.
  • Keep Emergency Alerts enabled in Settings so other drivers hear clear voice warnings.
 Tools & Features

Saved Places: Go to "Tools > Saved Places" to set your Home and Work addresses for one-tap navigation.

Dashcam: Access via the Tools menu. Connects to compatible WiFi dashcams to view and download footage.

Road Sign OCR: Use your camera to scan road signs. The app detects speed limits and warns you if you exceed them.

Pothole Detection: The app uses your phone's accelerometer to automatically detect severe bumps and report potential potholes.

 Settings & Customization

Profile: Update your username and view your Trust Score.

Mute Settings: Configure a mute radius (e.g., around your home) or specific streets where you don't want audio alerts.

Calibration: Use the Tools menu to calibrate the pothole detection sensitivity for your specific vehicle/mount.

Reporting Preferences: Turn on or off different types of reports (Accident, Pothole, Traffic, Police) according to your needs.

Emergency Alerts: Enable or disable spoken alerts for emergency vehicles reported nearby. If disabled, you will still see them on the map but not hear voice warnings.
