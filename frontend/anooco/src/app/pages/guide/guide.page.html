User Guide

Welcome to Anooco

Your intelligent road companion. Stay safe, informed, and connected.

Dashboard & Map

The Map: Shows your current location and nearby alerts (potholes, accidents, police, etc.).

View Modes: Use the Map / List / Split button in the bottom actions to toggle between:

  • Split: Map on top, alerts list below.
  • Map Only: Full-screen map (default when you open the app).
  • List Only: Full-screen list of alerts.

Settings & Tools: Use the gear icon in the top-left to open an action sheet for Settings and Tools.

Alerts Icon: The bell icon in the top-right opens the alerts list. The badge shows how many active alerts are nearby.

 Reporting Incidents

Voice Reporting: Tap the Voice button or say a wake word (if Auto On). Then say:

  • "Report accident"
  • "Report pothole"
  • "Report traffic"
  • "Report police"

Wake Words: With Hands-free (Auto) on, say "Hey Scout" or "Hey Beam" to wake the assistant.

Manual Reporting: Tap "Manual" to select an incident type.

Emergency Beam: Say "Hey Scout, Beam emergency ahead" to warn nearby drivers.

 Voice Assistant & Commands

Wake Words: Enable "Auto On" and say "Hey Scout" or "Hey Beam".

Reporting Commands:

  • Accidents: "Accident", "Crash", "Collision"
  • Hazards: "Pothole", "Bad road", "Roadwork", "Debris", "Slippery"
  • Police: "Police", "Speed camera", "Roadblock"
  • Traffic: "Traffic jam", "Stuck", "Slow"

Parking & Navigation:

  • "Park here" or "Save my car" (Saves location)
  • "Take me to my car" (Navigates to saved spot)

App Control:

  • "Scout pause" (Pause alerts)
  • "Scout resume" (Resume alerts)
 Tools & Features

Saved Places: Go to "Tools > Saved Places" to set your Home and Work addresses for one-tap navigation.

Dashcam: Access via the Tools menu. Connects to compatible WiFi dashcams to view and download footage.

Road Sign OCR: Use your camera to scan road signs. The app detects speed limits and warns you if you exceed them.

Pothole Detection: The app uses your phone's accelerometer to automatically detect severe bumps and report potential potholes.

 Settings

Map Style: Choose between Satellite, Dark, Light, or Outdoors map styles.

Units: Toggle between Metric (km) and Imperial (mi).

Notifications: Customize which alerts you want to receive and their distance radius.

Profile: Update your profile picture and vehicle details.
